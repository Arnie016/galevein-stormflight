Galevein: Stormflight changes:
- reduced embedded textures to a web-sized 1024-pixel maximum;
- exported WebP textures and Draco-compressed geometry;
- shortened the snout, neck, and tail;
- broadened the head and wing planform;
- reduced the dorsal and long-horned silhouette;
- changed the material response to matte blue-black hide;
- renamed selected control bones and three browser-authoritative clips;
- attached the original Galevein rider, wind-response, and gameplay systems.

The resulting character and game identity are called Sablewing and Galevein:
Stormflight. They are not presented as Night Fury or as an official character,
asset, or product from DreamWorks Animation.
